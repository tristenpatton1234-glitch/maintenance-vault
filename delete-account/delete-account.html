Delete Your MaintenanceVault Account

Donkey Jaw Services LLC

MaintenanceVault lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app, at any time, with no waiting period.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the MaintenanceVault app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top left to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete My Account & Data.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation field and tap the button to confirm.
This action is immediate and permanent. Deleting your account permanently removes your login credentials, profile information, and all maintenance logs from our cloud servers. This cannot be undone.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account credentials and email address
  • Your profile information (name, nickname, avatar)
  • All maintenance logs, equipment records, and notes stored in the cloud
  • Your subscription and purchase history association with your account

Need Help?

If you're having trouble accessing the app or completing this process, contact us at support@maintenancevaultapp.com and we'll assist you directly.
